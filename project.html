Barber Queue-Glowora

Glowora is a queue and booking app for salons and barber shops, serving both men and women. Customers can find a shop, join the queue or book a slot, and pay online through Razorpay or UPI. Barbers get their own dashboard to manage the live queue, bookings, services, staff and analytics, and can upload their shop photos and personal payment QR.

AI Attendance

The AI-Based Smart Attendance System uses face recognition to mark attendance automatically. A camera captures a student’s face, matches it with registered data, and logs the date and time in a database. It prevents proxy and duplicate entries, and an admin dashboard provides daily and monthly reports

FocusNext

FocusNext is a simple, distraction-free productivity app that helps you study and work with more focus. It combines a Pomodoro timer with task management, daily goals and a “distraction parking lot”, so stray thoughts get noted instead of pulling you away from what you’re doing.
